The Challenge

Edge Analytics needed a cohesive brand identity and digital experience that could communicate the company's work, people, and services clearly to its audience.

The challenge was to establish a distinctive visual identity while organizing complex company information into an intuitive website experience. The design needed to balance a professional brand presence with clear navigation and accessible content, helping visitors understand the company and find the information most relevant to them.

Research and Discovery

I began with extensive stakeholder discussions and collaborative sessions with project managers to better understand the company's identity, business objectives, and target clientele. These conversations explored how stakeholders viewed the company, what differentiated its services, how it wanted to be perceived within its industry, and what prospective clients needed to understand about its offerings.

A central focus of discovery was bringing clarity to the company's brand positioning. By examining stakeholder perspectives, business goals, and audience expectations, I worked to establish a cohesive understanding of the company's identity and the direction its brand should take. These findings informed the development of the visual identity and the website's overall design strategy.

Following design and development, I conducted usability research prior to launch to evaluate the website experience, assess how effectively users navigated the content, and identify opportunities for refinement. This provided an additional layer of validation, helping ensure the final experience was both aligned with the company's objectives and accessible to its intended audience.

The Solution: Building a brand with a sharper edge.

I worked closely with the project manager to translate the company's newly established brand direction into a cohesive visual identity. This included developing a custom logo, color palette, typography, and supporting iconography, creating a recognizable identity that better reflected the company's positioning and values.

With the brand established, I carried the visual language into the website design, focusing on clear information architecture, intuitive navigation, and consistent visual hierarchy. I developed the interface and supporting design system in Figma, creating reusable components and cohesive layouts that balanced the company's professional identity with an accessible user experience.

Final Design Impact

The project gave Edge Analytics a unified brand identity and a cohesive digital presence, establishing a clearer and more consistent way to communicate its value to prospective clients. By connecting stakeholder insights and brand strategy with thoughtful UX/UI design, I helped transform an undefined visual direction into a complete digital experience.

From initial brand discovery through usability research, interface design, and development collaboration, the project demonstrated the value of aligning business identity, audience needs, and digital design.
