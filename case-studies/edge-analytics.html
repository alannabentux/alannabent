Design System

Clarity, built into every detail.

A focused visual language pairs a bright data-inspired gradient with crisp typography, calm surfaces, and simple interface components.

Color palette · Select a swatch to explore its role in the interface.

Paired with deep blue or white for high contrast and emphasis.
