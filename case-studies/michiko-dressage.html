The Challenge

Michiko Smith Dressage is a boutique dressage and horse sales facility that needed to establish its first cohesive brand identity and digital presence.

With no pre-existing brand guidelines, visual identity, or established design direction, the project began with a blank slate. The challenge was not simply to create a website, but to define how the business would present itself visually and communicate its identity to prospective clients.

This required developing a brand from the ground up while translating the refinement and tradition of dressage into a modern, accessible digital experience. The resulting design needed to communicate professionalism and elegance while making the facility's services, offerings, and information easy to explore.

Research and Discovery

I began with extensive stakeholder interviews and client discussions to develop a deeper understanding of the business, its clientele, and the needs of its prospective customers. These conversations explored the characteristics of its client base, their expectations when evaluating a dressage facility, and the information and services most important to their decision-making.

This discovery process helped establish a clearer picture of the target audience and informed decisions around the website's content, organization, and overall user experience.

Alongside stakeholder research, I conducted a competitive analysis of other dressage and equestrian businesses, evaluating their websites for visual presentation, navigation, content hierarchy, and usability. This revealed opportunities to improve upon common industry design patterns and create a more intuitive, visually engaging experience.

Following development, I conducted usability testing with prospective users to evaluate the website experience, identify areas of friction, and make refinements before launch.

The Solution: A refined digital experience.

I developed a cohesive brand identity and digital experience from the ground up. Drawing on the elegance and tradition of dressage, I established a visual direction that balanced a refined equestrian aesthetic with a clean, contemporary feel. This included developing the color palette, typography, logo, and supporting iconography to create a consistent and recognizable brand.

I translated this identity into a responsive website, using the research findings to guide information architecture, navigation, and content hierarchy. The interface was designed to make services, sales offerings, and essential business information easy to discover while maintaining a visually engaging and cohesive experience.

The complete design system and website layouts were developed in Figma, establishing consistent visual elements and reusable interface components. These designs provided the foundation for the custom front-end development and responsive implementation.

Final Design Impact

The project established Michiko Smith Dressage's first cohesive brand identity and digital presence, bringing together a distinctive visual language and an intuitive, responsive website. By translating stakeholder insights and competitive research into thoughtful design decisions, the final experience provided a more cohesive way for prospective clients to explore the facility, its services, and its offerings.

From initial discovery and brand development through usability testing, custom development, and deployment, I delivered a complete end-to-end digital experience that aligned the business's identity with the needs of its audience.
