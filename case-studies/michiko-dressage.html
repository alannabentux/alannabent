Project Overview

A modern expression of equestrian elegance.

A boutique dressage and sales facility, Michiko Smith Dressage needed a cohesive online experience. The project brought together its visual identity and website through a focused design and development process.

Project Significance

Identifying the Need. Designing the Solution.

The challenge was to bring Michiko Smith Dressage’s equestrian character online while making its training, services, and sales offerings easy to discover.
The solution pairs an elegant equestrian identity with clear page organization and a custom menu, giving visitors direct paths through the facility’s website on desktop and mobile.
